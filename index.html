About

Background

I got into data science because I like using data to help people make better decisions — and increasingly, that includes decisions about the infrastructure behind AI itself, where compute, energy, and water are becoming real design constraints rather than afterthoughts.

At Data Science Alliance, I built Python pipelines feeding ML experimentation and folded LLM summarization into an existing analytics workflow, validating outputs through iterative error analysis. Volunteering with the Delta Rising Foundation, I applied the same rigor to geospatial data, helping identify sites for redwood reforestation and carbon sequestration.

I'm looking for a genuinely collaborative team that's dedicated to what the company is actually building — somewhere I can own real problems and grow fast.

Data Center Energy & Water Footprint Tracker

r = -0.50 correlation

Data centers get blamed for rising electric bills — I decided to test that claim instead of assuming it. Pulling public EIA and EPA data for Santa Clara County, I found something the popular narrative gets backwards: electricity use actually fell while prices nearly tripled, a statistically significant pattern that points to a very different culprit than "AI is spiking your bill."

Python · DuckDB · Streamlit · Prophet

Semiconductor Contamination & Vapor-Intrusion Risk Q&A

Local RAG · In progress

Santa Clara County has more EPA Superfund sites than anywhere else in the U.S. — most of them old chip fabs whose contamination is still being monitored decades later, buried in dense, scattered government records almost no one actually reads. I'm building a fully local AI system that can answer real questions about that risk directly from the source documents, no cloud, no shortcuts.

Python · Chroma · Hugging Face · Ollama · LoRA/PEFT

GitHub

San Diego MTS: Bus Network Optimization

-18% travel time

San Diego's buses move 200K+ riders a day, but nobody had actually modeled where the network was quietly failing them. I dug into real ridership and route data to find out — and turned it into three concrete route changes projected to cut travel times by 18%.

Python · Pandas · Matplotlib
